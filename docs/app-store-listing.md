## Description (4000 max)

```
Shuttler shows you exactly where your shuttle is, right now, and lets you request a pickup at your stop with one tap.

Shuttler is used by universities, schools, and organizations to run their shuttle service. Sign in through yours with your school email, SSO, Apple, Google, or phone number.

FOR RIDERS
• Live map: watch every shuttle move along its route in real time
• Request a pickup: tap a stop, see how far away the next bus is, and let the driver know you're waiting
• Know when to head out: get a notification when your bus is on the way, and the map zooms in as it arrives
• Routes tab: each route drawn on a map, with its stops in order and where the bus is right now
• Bus details: route, driver, next stops, seats available, and today's hours at a glance
• Service alerts for delays, detours, and schedule changes
• Ride history and accessibility settings for text size and motion

FOR PARENTS (K-12)
• Link your child's profile and follow their shuttle live
• Get notified when the bus is approaching their stop

FOR DRIVERS
• Start and end shifts with one tap; your location is shared only while you're on shift
• See riders waiting at each stop on your route
• Log boardings, take breaks, and update seat availability

FOR ADMINISTRATORS
• Live dashboard: who's driving, where riders are waiting, and today's numbers
• Driver details: shift history, breaks, boardings, and requests completed
• Org Setup: add stops on a map, build routes, set hours, and pick route colors; changes go live instantly
• Manage drivers, admins, and riders, and invite people to join
• Analytics, weekly AI-written insights, and CSV data export
• AI assistant for questions about your operation
• Single sign-on (SAML) for university and enterprise accounts

PRIVACY
Rider location is only used while the app is open, to show nearby stops and help you request a pickup. Drivers' location is shared only during an active shift. We never sell your data.

Shuttler requires an account with a participating organization. Check whether yours is on Shuttler at shuttler.net. Riders never pay: your school or organization covers the service, and there are no in-app purchases. If yours isn't on Shuttler yet, you can let us know there too.

Privacy Policy: https://shuttler.net/privacy
Terms of Use: https://shuttler.net/terms
Support: support@shuttler.net
```
